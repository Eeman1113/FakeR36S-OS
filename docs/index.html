FakeR36S-OS Installer

For fake R36S handhelds with a GA36-MB board and an Allwinner A23 chip. Your SD card image is built right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

↻ Already running FakeR36S-OS? Update instead

Updating keeps everything of yours: games, saves and save states, RetroArch and per-core settings, button remaps, themes, box art, BIOS and ports. No reflash, no copying games again.

  1. Download the update package (fakers36s-update-a23_panel2.tar), or let this page try to fetch it.
  2. Plug the console's card into this computer, then put the package on its HOME drive with the button below.
  3. Eject the card, put it back in the console and power on. Your logo and spinner stay up for a few minutes while it installs, then it restarts into the new version. The log is in HOME/.config/fakers36s/update.log.
Without Chrome or Edge

Make a folder named update in the root of the card's HOME drive and copy the downloaded .tar into it, unchanged. Then eject the card and power on the console. On macOS or Linux you can also run tools/update-os.sh from the repo to write the new system straight to the card.

1 Back up your stock SD card

The card that came with the console holds its bootloader. Without it, the console can't boot anything. Make a backup file, then keep the original card safe and untouched.

macOS 
diskutil list external physical        # find your card, e.g. /dev/disk4
sudo dd if=/dev/rdisk4 of=stock_backup.img bs=4m status=progress
Linux 
lsblk                                   # find your card, e.g. /dev/sdb
sudo dd if=/dev/sdb of=stock_backup.img bs=4M status=progress
Windows

Use Win32 Disk Imager: choose the card, name the file stock_backup.img, click Read. If Windows asks to format the card, click Cancel.

Only the first 128 MB is needed, but a full backup also keeps your games and saves. Full guide

2 Choose your backup

Pick stock_backup.img. The installer reads 128 KB of it to identify your board.

3 Build your image

Downloads FakeR36S-OS (about 150 MB), adds your card's boot region and the boot logo.

Automatic download didn't work in this browser. Download goodluck_base_a23_panel2.img.gz yourself, then pick it here:

4 Flash a new microSD card

Save fakers36s.img, then write it to a new card with balenaEtcher or Raspberry Pi Imager (Choose OS, then Use custom).

Save fakers36s.img

Put the card in slot TF1 and power on. The first boot takes a little longer while it grows the games partition. Then copy your games to the HOME partition, in roms/<system>/.

5 Add box art automatically

Plug the card back into this computer and pick its HOME drive. Every game without art gets box art from the libretro thumbnail library, added straight to EmulationStation. Art you already have is left alone.

For menu music, drop .mp3 or .ogg files into roms/bgmusic.

No BIOS files or games are included. Use your own legally obtained copies.
